Our Concept

One wardrobe. Three ways to wear it.

Stocka lets you decide how long fashion stays with you — for a moment, for a season, or for good.

Stocka Loop — Rent

Wear it for now. Return it when you’re done.

How it works

  • Select Rent on an eligible item
  • Choose your rental period
  • Pick a pickup date at checkout
  • Your rental starts when you collect the item

Need more time? You can extend your rental anytime from your account.

Good to know:

  • Rentals begin after pickup, not at checkout
  • Items must be picked up within the specified pickup window
  • If an item isn’t collected in time, the rental is automatically cancelled
  • Each item is rented individually, so availability stays clear and fair

Stocka Keep — Buyout

Love it? Make it yours.

If you rented an item and decide to keep it, you can buy it out at any time.

How it works:

  • Your previous rental payments are credited toward the final price
  • You pay only the remaining value to own the item fully
  • No extra steps — just a simple checkout

Nothing feels wasted. Your rental simply evolves into ownership.

Stocka Reuse — Pre-owned

Second life. Same standards.

Pre-owned items are carefully selected pieces offered at a reduced price.

How it works:

  1. Choose Pre-owned on eligible items
  2. Purchase at a discounted price
  3. The item is yours to keep

Good to know:

  • Pre-owned items are final sale
  • Each piece is curated for quality, fit, and continued wear
  • No returns or exchanges

This keeps pricing fair and supports a more circular way to shop. Thoughtful by design

Stocka is built around flexibility and longevity — not fast fashion. Choose what fits your life now, and change your mind later.

About our program names

Stocka Loop (Rent), Stocka Reuse (Pre-owned), and Stocka Keep (Rental buyout). They help explain your options — the shopping experience stays seamless.
