Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 10, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Stocka (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our platform.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide voluntarily, such as your:

  • Name, email, and payment details when placing an order
  • Contact information through our forms or messages
  • Preferences and sign-ups for updates or newsletters

We might also collect non-personal information for analytics and performance, such as browser type, device, and IP address.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information to:

Stocka uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience and analyze usage. You may disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not function properly.

We may share your information with trusted third-party services (e.g., payment processors, delivery providers, analytics tools) strictly to operate the platform. All partners follow strict data protection standards.

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

Stocka is not intended for users under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
