Returns & Refunds

Effective date: June 20, 2024

Stocka is built around flexibility and circular fashion. Because our products include rentals and pre-owned items, return rules vary by option. Please read carefully before placing an order.

Buy New

Standard return policy applies.

Brand-new items can be returned within the specified return window, provided they are:

  • Unworn
  • Unwashed
  • In original condition with tags attached

Return eligibility and timelines are shown at checkout and in your order confirmation.

Stocka Loop — Rentals

Rentals are non-refundable.

Once a rental is confirmed:

  • Rental payments are not refunded
  • Rental periods begin after item pickup
  • Unused rental time cannot be refunded

This policy ensures fair availability and supports the shared nature of rental inventory.

Good to know:

  • You can extend an active rental at any time
  • You can request a size replacements right away
  • If you love the item, rental payments can be credited toward ownership through Stocka Keep

Stocka Reuse — Pre-owned

All pre-owned purchases are final sale.

Because pre-owned items are carefully curated one-of-a-kind pieces:

  • No exchanges
  • No returns
  • No refunds

Each pre-owned item is reviewed for quality and wear before being listed.

Order Issues & Exceptional Cases

We know that sometimes things don’t go exactly as planned. Stuff happens — and when it does, we’re genuinely sorry. If something feels off with your order, we’re here to help and make it right.

Refunds may be issued only in the following situations:

  • You were charged more than once for the same order
  • A confirmed order could not be fulfilled due to a system or processing error on our end

In these cases, please contact us within 7 days of purchase.
