Know before you go

How long does shipping take?

Most orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Delivery typically takes 3–7 business days, depending on your location and selected shipping method.

Where can I shop Stocka?

Stocka is online-only — intentionally. This allows us to curate thoughtfully, reduce waste, and keep the experience focused and flexible. No stores. No queues. Just fashion on your terms, wherever you are.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Returns depend on purchase type: Buy New items are accepted within the return window if unworn; Rent (Stocka Loop) payments are non-refundable; Pre-owned (Stocka Reuse) purchases are final sale.

I ordered the wrong size. What should I do?

For new items, you can request a size exchange through your account, subject to availability. For rentals, we recommend checking sizing carefully before checkout, as rental payments cannot be refunded.

When does my rental start?

Your rental begins when you pick up the item, not when you place the order. This ensures you get the full rental period — no time lost in transit.

Can I extend my rental?

Yes. You can extend an active rental at any time from your account, as long as the item remains available. Each extension is handled as a simple, separate checkout.

What if I want to keep a rented item?

You can buy it out anytime through Stocka Keep. Your previous rental payments are credited toward the final price — so nothing feels wasted, just evolved.

What does "Pre-owned" mean?

Pre-owned items are carefully selected pieces offered at a reduced price. Each item is reviewed for quality, wear, and continued use before being listed.

Can I return a pre-owned item?

No. All pre-owned purchases are final sale. This keeps pricing fair and supports a more circular fashion model.

What is Stocka?

Stocka is a modern fashion marketplace that lets you buy new, rent, or shop pre-owned — all in one place. It's built for flexibility, longevity, and better decision-making.

What do Stocka Loop, Reuse, and Keep mean?

These are labels for your choices: Stocka Loop is for Renting, Stocka Reuse is for Pre-owned, and Stocka Keep is for Rental buyout. The experience stays seamless across all options.
